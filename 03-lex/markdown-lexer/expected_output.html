Typography and Text Styling

Here is a paragraph demonstrating italic text with asterisks, italic with underscores, bold text with asterisks, and bold with underscores.

You can also combine formatting: bold and italic combined, or write strikethrough text with tildes. Mathematical expressions like 5 * 10 = 50 and identifiers like user_id_count remain unaffected by emphasis rules.

Inline code is protected: int count = (x < 10 && y > 20); with escaped entities.
